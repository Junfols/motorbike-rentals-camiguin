Island Adventure Guide

Where to Go in Camiguin & Best Bike Match

Discover curated island routes and the recommended motorbike type for the smoothest, safest ride.

🌊 Coastal Loop (64 km)

Circumferential Highway Scenic Tour

Smooth, fully paved 2-lane coastal highway encircling the entire island. Pass through Mambajao, Catarman, Sagay, Guinsiliban, and Mahinog with open ocean vistas on one side and volcanic slopes on the other.

Key Highlights: Sunken Cemetery, Guiob Church Ruins, White Island Sandbar, Walkway to the Old Volcano, Soda Water Pool.
Recommended Bike:
110cc - 125cc Scooter (Click 125i, Mio, Beat)

Lightweight, ultra fuel-efficient (up to 50 km/L), and effortless twist-and-go automatic riding for relaxed coastal cruising.

💧 Waterfalls & Springs

Katibawasan & Tuasan Mountain Ascent

Scenic mountain roads ascending towards Katibawasan Falls (76-meter drop) and Tuasan Falls. Paved roads with occasional steep climbs and refreshing mountain mist.

Key Highlights: Katibawasan Falls, Tuasan Falls, Ardent Hibok-Hibok Hot Spring, Sto. Niño Cold Spring.
Recommended Bike:
150cc - 160cc Maxi Scooter (NMAX 155, PCX 160, ADV 160)

Extra torque for steep uphill climbs with a pillion passenger, plush dual rear suspension, and large under-seat storage for swimwear & towels.

🌋 Volcano Pass

Cross-Island Trans-Mountain Highway

Dramatic trans-island mountain pass cutting through the heart of Camiguin between Mt. Hibok-Hibok and Mt. Timpoong. Features high elevation, sharp switchbacks, and cool mountain air.

Key Highlights: Camiguin Highlands 360° viewpoint, lush rainforest canopy, misty ridges, shortcut between Mambajao & Sagay.
Recommended Bike:
Dual-Sport / Adventure Bike (CRF150L, WR155, ADV)

High ground clearance, aggressive suspension travel, and dual-purpose tires for maximum control on steep mountain switchbacks.

🏝️ Marine Sanctuary

Mantigue Island & East Coast Trail

Relaxed seaside route leading along Mahinog and San Roque heading to the Mantigue Island boat jump-off point and giant clam sanctuary.

Key Highlights: Mantigue Island Nature Reserve, Benoni Lagoon, Taguines Lagoon Zipline, J&A Fishpen, Kibila Giant Clam Sanctuary.
Recommended Bike:
Any Scooter or Big Bike (All Categories)

Gentle coastal roads with easy parking at ports and boat terminals.
